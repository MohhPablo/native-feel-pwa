# Compact reports

## Component reuse record (before implementation)
- Date, task, requirements, project/framework versions:
- Existing component/imports/design tokens inspected:
- Candidates + official docs/version/license URLs + maintenance evidence:
- Hard gates: behaviour, accessibility, license, framework (pass/fail/unknown):
- Bundle delta: measured, estimate or unknown; method:
- Decision and smallest adaptation; why alternatives lose:
- New dependencies/authorization; copied-source attribution:
- Verification plan and device gaps:

## Audit finding
`ID | severity (critical/high/medium/low) | confirmed/candidate | path:line | evidence/reproduction | impact | minimal remedy | regression check`
Prioritize security/data loss, accessibility blockers and lifecycle breakage before cosmetic polish. Do not call a regex match a confirmed runtime defect. Record scan exclusions and untested routes.

Use NOT RUN, not PASS, for expectations.
